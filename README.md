# Power BI Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze business sales performance and generate meaningful insights from sales data.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of:

* Total Sales
* Profit Analysis
* Sales Trends
* Regional Performance
* Customer Segments
* Category-wise Sales Distribution

The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into visually appealing and interactive business insights that help in better decision-making.

---

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard includes important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

* Total Sales: **$2M**
* Average Sales: **$229.9**
* Total Cost: **₹2.0M**
* Total Profit: **$286K**

### Sales by Category

Analyzes sales contribution from different product categories:

* Technology
* Furniture
* Office Supplies

### Sales by Segment

Displays sales distribution across customer segments:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

### Sales by Region

Compares regional sales performance:

* West
* East
* Central
* South

### Sales Trend by Year

Visualizes yearly sales growth from **2014 to 2017** using a line chart.

### Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis

Compares monthly sales and profit performance to identify business growth patterns and profitable periods.

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for year-based filtering, enabling users to interact dynamically with the report.

## Key Insights

* The **Consumer** segment generated the highest sales revenue.
* The **West** region recorded the highest sales performance.
* Sales showed continuous growth from **2015 to 2017**.
* Technology products contributed significantly to overall sales.
* Higher sales months also reflected improved profit margins.
